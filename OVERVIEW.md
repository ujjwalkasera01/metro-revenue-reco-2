# Metro Revenue Reconciliation (MetroRev)

A frontend-only prototype of a revenue reconciliation and AI operations console for a metro rail network, built for UI review and sign-off.

**Who it's for:** metro Station, AFC, Revenue and Finance teams, plus Administrators. Each role gets its own dashboard and permissions; the login screen offers demo access for every role, and you can switch roles from the profile menu.

**What it covers:** an executive dashboard, a calendar-style reconciliation monitor (AFC vs station figures, with drill-down), discrepancy ticketing with SLAs, a simulated AI calling and communication center, final settlement reports with PDF/CSV export, analytics, station/SPOC configuration, audit logs, settings, and user management.

**Important:** all data, calls, emails, sign-in and approvals are simulated in the browser using sample data for 10 stations across the Blue and Green lines (amounts in INR). Nothing connects to a real backend or service.
